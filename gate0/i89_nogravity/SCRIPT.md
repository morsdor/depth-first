## 3. The G4 checklist from the skill

- **ONE ruler.** The reel's ruler is **how hard gravity pulls, as a share of the ground's** — 88% (beat 1), 150→132 lb (beat 2), 99.7% (beat 4), the 88% arrow (beat 8). **Two numbers answer a different question and each appears once, with its unit on its object:** distance from the centre (beat 3, the "why not zero") and speed (beat 6, the "how can it fall and not land"). That is more than one ruler. **I am naming it rather than hiding it: the r009 verdict was "we don't know what we are comparing against", and the defence here is that both extra numbers explain the one ruler, never compete with it.** Beat 3 is the cut if the owner disagrees.
- **The hook SHOWS the result.** Station moving at 0.3 s; "88%" on screen by 1.5 s.
- **Names ride on the objects.** "THE STATION" on the station, the mph labels on the balls, "+7%" on the band, "THOUGHT EXPERIMENT" on the tower.
- **Never more than three text blocks at once.** The densest moments are beat 3 (two blocks) and beat 6 (three: two mph labels and "NO AIR").
- **No jargon.** "Parabola", "microgravity", "orbit", "free fall", "apoapsis", "reduced-gravity" never appear. "Curve", "falls", "floats".
- **Twelve distinct numbers** in 42.5 s: 88, 150, 132, 260, 7, 6, 99.7, 20, 6,700, 17,000, 14, 93. **That is a lot**, and it is the second thing the owner may want to cut (§4).
- **Every claim traced:** every figure to `measure.py` and `figures.json`; the plane, the altitude and "about 90%" to search-level sources in `research/RESEARCH.md`, none of them read at the primary page.
- **Every mechanism sentence falsifiable, and falsified-or-not:** "at 17,000 mph it never lands" → integrated ten orbits, radius drift 0.0 m, landed = False; "slower and it lands" → 0.95× lands. "Falls 14 ft, the ground curves away 14 ft" → equal to 1%. "A plane at 6 miles feels 99.7%" → 99.69%, computed.
- **Safe area, ground, breathing:** the build uses `ReelGround`, `useBreath`, the `SAFE` constants; none of that is decided here.

## 5. 3D — Stage 3c, the shots that only exist because it is 3D

- **The tower ascent and pull-back (beats 2→3).** The camera rides a bathroom scale 260 miles up a tower and then pulls back until the whole planet fits and the station's ring hugs it. One continuous move, no cut: **a change of scale from a scale to a planet**, which 2D cannot do.

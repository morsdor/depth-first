## THE ONE RULER

**The only measured quantity on screen is HEIGHT ABOVE SEA LEVEL, in feet.** It appears as one
readout, which reads 29,032 FT in the hook and payoff and climbs to it in the collision beat.

Two other things on screen are not on that ruler, and each is deliberately kept off it:
- **Ages** ("450 million years ago", "50 million years ago") label *when*. They are never compared
  with each other or plotted.
- **Speed is never a number.** It is said only against the viewer's own fingernails: about 4× while
  India is racing, about 1× today. No cm/yr, no inches per year, no miles.

**The copy column read alone, with the other columns covered (the r010 check):**

> The top of Everest used to be seafloor. The rock up there is full of fossil sea creatures,
> collected 20 feet below the summit. It formed 450 million years ago on the floor of a warm,
> shallow sea. Then India broke away and raced north across an ocean, four times faster than your
> fingernails grow. 50 million years ago it hit Asia, and the seafloor was crumpled and stacked
> into mountains. From the seafloor to 29,032 feet. India is still pushing into Asia at about the
> speed your fingernails grow.

That is a complete thought, and no beat needs a picture to make sense. **It never mentions the
flood, the Bible or creationism.** The mechanism is the answer, and the viewer carries it.

**Text blocks on screen at once:** never more than three (copy, the height readout, and in the race
beat the year clock).

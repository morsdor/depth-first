## 3. The G4 checklist from the skill

- **ONE ruler.** *Is the sun up at this clock time?* — 8:27, 7:27, "5 P.M.", "8 A.M.". Calendar dates (Jan 6, Oct 5) are the story's timeline, not a measurement, and appear once each.

- **Eight distinct figures** in 40 s: five clock times (7:00, 8:27, 7:27, 5 P.M., 8 A.M.) and three dates (Jan 7, Jan 6 and Oct 5, all 1974). That is fewer than `I89`'s twelve and still enough that **the owner's cut list is §4 item 4.**

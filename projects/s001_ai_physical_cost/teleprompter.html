Teleprompter — how to drive it

SpacePlay / pause the scroll
↑ ↓Speed up / down (words-per-minute)
+ −Bigger / smaller text
← →Nudge back / forward while paused
R / HomeJump back to the top
Time barDrag the bottom bar — or scroll the text — to jump to any section
FFullscreen
MMirror (for a beam-splitter glass rig)
NShow / hide scene directions
HYellow highlight on the spoken lines
GShow / hide the read-line guide
? / EscThis help / close

Speed is words-per-minute and re-calibrates to your font size automatically — set it near your real pace (~145), hit Space, and read to the marked line. Blue section bars with a 🎙 are the beats to make your own. Everything you change is remembered on this machine.

## Staging changes made while building — none touches the copy, each is flagged

These are build-time staging decisions inside the approved beats. None adds, removes or rewords a line
of copy; each was forced by something seen in a rendered still.

1. **The meters fade in at 1.4 s, not 2.5 s**, and read **33% / 36% at 8 AM** (gas since midnight), not
   0%. The lower half of the hook frame was empty, and a meter counting from 8 AM would have put the
   headline on a different base than the 9% that is claimed (100% = the held house's whole *day*).
2. **The clock lives in a slot of its own** between the houses and the meters (y 1026), and **the lead
   line and the "SIMULATED · ONE WINTER DAY" tag take the clock's place** when their beats arrive. It sat
   on top of the roofs everywhere else.
3. **The thermostat labels sit above each roof**, not on the dial: centred on the dial, "DROPS TO 62°" ran
   past the safe edge (x ≈ 900).
4. **The close-up (INTO) shot is scale 1.5, not 2.05**, so the roof clears the headline; the parked family
   car fades out in close-ups, where it covered the clock.
5. **The leak arrows leave each house on its own side** (left house through its left wall, right house
   through its right) and through both roof slopes — the first version pointed the left house's arrows
   *into* the right-hand house.
6. **The heat-pump beat is house-scale on the right-hand house only**, with the backup strip drawn in the
   indoor air handler (where the resistance strip physically is) at the furnace's position. At the
   draft's small scale the strip was invisible and lit for 1.5 s; the replay now runs 5:00 → 6:00 PM over
   3.0 s. The held house's dark strip is no longer shown.
7. **The held (left-hand) house leaves frame when the close-up move begins (14.0 s) and returns once the pull-back has settled
   (25.6 s), and leaves again before the heat-pump move (35.3 s).** Mid-move its left wall sits under x = 60 and a half-faded house
   still draws bright edges there, so it is windowed on time, not faded on zoom. The meters keep both houses present throughout.
8. **The last caption holds to the final frame** (40.0 s) instead of fading out in the last five. With no close, the heat-pump
   line is the reel's last word and it keeps its whole beat.
9. **The family car drives a short way and fades**, not off the side of the frame, which put its edges in the unsafe margin.

## Reading it, at GATE 5 and after

The reel is *small by nature* (8–9% on a cold day; ~5% over a winter), so its job is the correction,
not the number. Per Gate 0 §2, read **shares** (argument) and **saves** (utility) separately and expect
`r016`'s band, not `r005`'s. **Pre-registered, proposed — adjustable by the owner before posting, never
after:** shares ≥ **0.223%** (`r016` day 2) is the upper band; ≤ **0.124%** (`r010`) is no better than the
floor; saves ≥ **0.43%** (`r016` day 2) means utility travelled. Average watch is **not** the test — the
account's 16–18 s is the constraint the script was built around, and the verdict is on screen by 14 s.
**Two confounds to register now:** no close means no follow ask (follows are expected to be ~0 whatever
the reel does), and an unread-source reel cannot borrow "a study found" credibility.

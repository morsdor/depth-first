# Systems-lane outliers — small channels with real views

*Generated 2026-10-07 by `scripts/small_channel_hunt.py` (YouTube Data API v3) and hand-vetted for lane relevance. Source data: `data/small_channel_hits_systems.csv`, `data/lane_discovery_v2.csv`.*

**What this is:** videos from channels under 5,000 subscribers (tier A) or 5,000–10,000 (tier B) that reached 12,000+ views, published since 2024-01-01, on-lane = how computing physically works, how AI actually works, building/measuring things, algorithms shown visually.
**What it is not:** a base rate. Search was ranked by views, the 40 queries are mine, and subscriber counts are today's. It proves these results exist, not that they are typical. Company channels (Crusoe AI, BIZON, Applied Digital, Scan Business) were removed — their views look promoted.
